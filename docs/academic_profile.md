# Reasearch

## Research Interest

Broadly, my research lies at the intersection of theoretical computer science, operations research, and economics. My primary focus is on online optimization and mechanism design, particularly developing algorithms for dynamic resource allocation and decision-making under uncertainty in online markets.

More recently, I have been exploring **AI for theoretical research**—investigating how automated theorem-proving agents reason, and designing architectures that enable deeper, more general, and provably reliable mathematical thinking.
